# Brand and interface reference - 2026-10-26

## Status

Reference input only. This is not an accepted product name, logo, exact palette, typography system, or implementation specification, and it is not product canon until reconciled through the normal design and canon process.

## Source

## Direction retained

- The author likes the overall color direction and warmth.
- Useful palette cues include deep forest green, rust or terracotta, warm camel or muted gold, parchment or cream, sage, and blue-gray, with restrained navy, olive, ochre, pale gray, and blush as possible supporting colors.
- The combination suggests a warm, archival, literary, multigenerational character that may inform later Reader, Story Creation, and Archive Management exploration.

## Explicitly not adopted

- `TOLD` is not an available product name and is not adopted.
- None of the three marks or their subordinate variants is selected.
- The displayed type treatments and slogans are not approved product copy.
- The displayed colors are visual references, not yet sampled or promoted into theme tokens.

## Interface feedback retained with this reference

The first mobile-author, desktop-archive, and mobile-reader mockups showed individual working screens without enough preamble, orientation, navigation, or surrounding journey. Future interface exploration should show the contextual pages and transitions that answer:

- Where am I in the product and in my life-story structure?
- Why is this the next task?
- What came before this screen?
- What happens after I finish it?
- How do Stories, Chapters, Archive work, family reading, and incomplete work fit together?

This feedback does not reject the task-focused direction. It requires the next interface artifact to present the whole product-level experience architecture first, then use several representative journeys to test how those task screens fit within it. No single recent-event, archive, spoken-memory, object-led, or Reader journey should become the organizing frame for the product.
